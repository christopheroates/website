Chris Oates

I lead a team working in the areas of Computational Statistics and Probabilistic Machine Learning at Newcastle University in the UK.

Recent News

  • New monograph: Probabilistic Inference and Learning with Stein's Method
  • I am proud to have supervised the ISBA Savage Award winner!
  • New podcast: The Indecisive Statistician, on the Numberphile podcast.
  • New video: Stein's Paradox, on the Numberphile channel.
  • For 2025-2028 I am serving on the Programme Committee of the ICMS.
  • For 2025-2027 I am serving as an Associate Editor of the SIAM/ASA Journal of Uncertainty Quantification.
  • New book: Scalable Monte Carlo for Bayesian Learning
  • I am delighted to be awarded the Guy Medal in Bronze from the Royal Statistical Society!
  • I have been awarded a Leverhulme Prize, which will support the team's activities in 2024-2027.
